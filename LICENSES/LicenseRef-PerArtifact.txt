LicenseRef-PerArtifact

Files under runs/raw/ and runs/normalized/ (both contain model output) are not licensed by this repository as a whole.

Each raw run record carries its own `licensing` block, for example:

  licensing:
    repository_license: NOASSERTION
    output_rights_basis: provider_terms
    provider: <provider>
    provider_terms_version: <date or version of the terms reviewed>
    redistributable: <true | false>

The rights in a model's output depend on the provider's terms at the time of
the run, and any quoted third-party text keeps its own terms. Read the
`licensing` block of the specific artifact before reusing it. Where
`redistributable` is false, the artifact must not be published in a release.

Hand-authored scenarios, annotations and scoring records (runs/annotations/, runs/scores/) are
licensed CC-BY-4.0 (see REUSE.toml); code is Apache-2.0.
